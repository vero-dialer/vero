Account control

Delete your VERO account

Request deletion of your VERO online account and associated server data. Removing VERO from your phone does not automatically delete the online account.

Last updated 12 November 2026 · vero.about.help@gmail.com

Delete from the VERO app

  1. Open VERO and tap Settings.
  2. Under VERO private calling, tap Manage VERO account.
  3. Tap Delete entire VERO account.
  4. Verify the request using the OTP sent to a linked number and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Submit the support form or email VERO using the address associated with your request. State that you want to delete your VERO account and provide only the last four digits of the verified number. Do not send an OTP, password, full message history or complete call log.

Open deletion request formEmail VERO

Identity verification: VERO may require fresh OTP verification before acting on a request. Knowing a phone number alone is not sufficient to delete another person’s account.

Data deleted or disconnected

  • VERO account identifiers and linked verified-number records.
  • Active device sessions, refresh credentials and FCM routing tokens.
  • Online call-reason routing records and account entitlements, subject to the retention exceptions below.

Data not deleted by this request

  • Contacts, call logs and SMS/MMS stored by Android, your carrier or another app.
  • Records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, payment accounting or legal compliance.
  • Protected backups until they are overwritten within the retention period described in the Privacy Policy.

Approved deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Protected backups are overwritten within 90 days. Google may apply separate retention periods to Firebase Authentication data.

For full details, read the VERO Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
