Account and data deletion

Delete Your VERO Account

Request permanent deletion of your VERO account and associated server-side personal data.

Effective 11 November 2026Contact: vero.about.help@gmail.com

You can request deletion even if VERO is no longer installed.

Use the support form or email below. Do not send an OTP, password, complete message history or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Request account deletion

  1. Open the VERO support form and enter Account deletion request as the request type or message subject.
  2. Provide the verified VERO number in the format requested by the form and an email address where we can contact you.
  3. Complete the ownership-verification step sent by VERO. We will never ask you to send us your OTP.
  4. After verification, we will confirm that deletion has started and notify you when it is complete.

Data deleted with the account

  • Encrypted verified phone-number records and active phone lookup mappings.
  • VERO account profile and server-side verified-number associations.
  • Firebase Authentication account associated with VERO, where controlled by VERO.
  • FCM routing token, registered-device credentials and active sessions.
  • Online call-reason routing records and server-stored preferences associated with the account.

Limited information we may retain

VERO does not keep the complete account merely by marking it deleted. We may retain a minimal deletion audit, security logs, fraud-prevention information or records required by law. Retained information is access-restricted and is not used to rebuild the account, advertise to you or create a contact database.

  • Security logs may be retained for 180 days, or longer only for an active security investigation or legal requirement.
  • A minimal deletion record may contain an anonymous request reference, request and completion dates, request channel, completion status and policy version.
  • Protected backups are overwritten through the normal backup cycle, ordinarily within 90 days.

Processing time

We aim to acknowledge a complete request within 7 days and complete verified deletion within 30 days. A request may take longer where identity cannot be verified, a security investigation is active or retention is required by law. We will explain any material delay where legally permitted.

Information remaining on the device

Deleting a VERO online account does not automatically delete contacts, Android call logs, SMS/MMS messages or blocked-number records controlled by Android, your mobile network or another application. Clear VERO app data or uninstall VERO to remove remaining app-local information. Keep any information you need before deletion because deleted VERO account data cannot be restored.

Questions

For deletion or privacy questions, email vero.about.help@gmail.com or use the VERO support form.
