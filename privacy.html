Privacy Policy

How VERO handles device information, account verification, permissions and connected services.

Effective 11 November 2026Contact: vero.about.help@gmail.com

Effective date: 11 November 2026 Last updated: 11 November 2026

SCOPE

This Privacy Policy explains how VERO, provided under the VERO name ("VERO", "we", "us" or "our"), accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when you use the VERO Android app, website, support channels and related online services.

VERO is designed so that ordinary contact, call-log and message processing occurs primarily on your device. Certain optional online features require limited server processing, as explained below.

INFORMATION PROCESSED ON YOUR DEVICE

Depending on permissions and features you enable, VERO may access and process locally:

  • Contacts: names, numbers and contact photos used to identify callers, search contacts, save new

    • contacts and show frequent contacts.

  • Call information: incoming, outgoing and missed-call records, timestamps, duration, call direction,

    • SIM/carrier labels and call state used for recents, dialling, reminders and call controls.

  • Messages: SMS/MMS sender, recipient, message content, timestamps and thread metadata used when VERO

    • is the default SMS app.

  • Microphone input: short audio input used for enabled voice commands and speech recognition.
  • Spam and classification data: numbers marked or unmarked as spam, local reputation information,

    • blocked-number choices and deterministic message classifications.

  • Preferences: ringtone, vibration, speaker/audio-route, auto-reply, reminder, short-dial, focus-mode,

    • intent-muting and other settings.

  • Phone and SIM information: active subscription/SIM metadata made available by Android, used to label

    • and place calls or messages through the selected line.

  • Installed messaging-app availability: whether supported apps such as WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or

    • Telegram can handle an action. VERO uses this to show available shortcuts.

VERO does not upload your address book to build a caller database. VERO does not upload ordinary call logs or complete SMS/MMS content to create advertising profiles. Local information may leave the device only when you initiate or enable a feature that requires transmission, such as placing a call, sending an SMS, submitting support information or using an online VERO call reason.

Local information remains subject to Android, device backup and manufacturer behaviour. Clearing app data or uninstalling VERO may remove local settings, spam records, reminders and other local history.

INFORMATION USED FOR VERO ACCOUNTS AND ONLINE FEATURES

When you verify a number or use online VERO features, we may process:

  • Your verified mobile number in international format.
  • Firebase Authentication user identifier and verification status.
  • A random VERO account identifier.
  • Protected phone-number lookup identifiers and, where necessary, an encrypted phone number.
  • Masked phone-number suffixes used for account display and support.
  • Random installation/device identifier created by VERO; VERO does not use the IMEI as its account ID.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to route time-sensitive notifications to the active installation.
  • Hashed device credentials or public-key information used to authenticate an installation.
  • App version, Android version, device model label and service-compatibility information.
  • Account status, verification time, account-access status and policy-acceptance versions.
  • Session, security and anti-abuse information, including timestamps, rate-limit events and coarse

    • network/security metadata where necessary.

  • Support requests and information you voluntarily submit through the support form.

OTP VERIFICATION AND FIREBASE

VERO uses Firebase Phone Authentication to send and verify OTPs. The phone number, IP address, user agent and related security information may be processed by Google to provide authentication and prevent abuse. Phone numbers provided for Firebase authentication are sent to and stored by Google for authentication and spam/abuse prevention.

Firebase, not VERO, generates and validates the OTP. VERO does not receive or store the OTP itself. After successful verification, VERO's server verifies the Firebase identity token before creating or restoring the VERO account.

Firebase services may process data using Google's global infrastructure. See Google's Firebase privacy and security information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

CALL REASONS AND QUICK REPLIES

When a caller selects a predefined VERO call reason

  • VERO sends a compact predefined reason identifier and minimum routing information through VERO's

    • secure service and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

  • The recipient's VERO app converts the identifier into the predefined text displayed in the app.
  • Call-reason delivery is temporary and retained only as long as necessary to route the reason to the

    • recipient.

  • The server may temporarily process protected sender and recipient routing identifiers to deliver and

    • match the reason to an incoming call.

  • A recipient may choose a predefined reply. If sent by SMS, the mobile network processes that SMS and

    • carrier charges may apply.

Where the caller enables missed-call SMS fallback, VERO may send the selected predefined reason as a standard SMS to a non-VERO recipient after an eligible unanswered call. This occurs from the user's SIM and does not require the recipient to have VERO. Users can disable this feature.

WHY VERO REQUESTS PERMISSIONS

Phone and call control

To place, receive, answer, reject and manage calls and display call state.

Call Log

To show recent calls, update call outcomes, support reminders, frequent contacts and local spam tools. VERO must be the active default Phone handler before using restricted Call Log permissions.

Contacts

To display saved names and photos, search contacts and save contacts. Contacts are not uploaded to build a global caller database.

SMS and MMS

To receive, display, organize and send messages when VERO is the active default SMS handler, and to send user-enabled call replies or missed-call reason SMS. Carrier charges may apply.

Microphone

To listen for enabled call-action voice commands. VERO does not use this permission for call recording.

Notifications and full-screen incoming-call display

To alert you to incoming calls, missed calls, messages, call-reason replies and reminders, including when the app is not in the foreground or the device is locked.

Bluetooth/nearby devices and audio settings

To identify and route call audio to a connected Bluetooth device, speaker or earpiece.

Phone state, phone numbers and SIM information: To identify available calling accounts/SIMs, show carrier labels and use the line selected for a call or message where Android provides that information.

Vibration, wake lock and boot completion: To vibrate for calls/alerts, keep essential call handling active and restore scheduled local reminders after a device restart.

Internet

To perform OTP verification, receive Firebase notifications, deliver online VERO call reasons, check account access and communicate with VERO support/services.

PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

We process information only as reasonably necessary to

  • Provide dialling, calling, messaging and enabled assistance features.
  • Display contact names, recent calls, message threads and selected SIM/carrier information.
  • Verify accounts and restore eligible access after a device change.
  • Deliver time-limited VERO call reasons and related notifications.
  • Detect abuse, protect accounts, enforce rate limits and investigate security incidents.
  • Diagnose compatibility and service-delivery problems.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, telecom, platform and app-store obligations.

VERO does not

  • Sell your phone number.
  • Sell your contacts.
  • Sell your call logs.
  • Sell message content.
  • Build advertising profiles from restricted Phone, SMS or Call Log permissions.

VERO uses certain third-party services, including Firebase and Android system services. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may be disclosed only as necessary to

  • Google/Firebase, for phone authentication, push delivery, app integrity and related infrastructure.
  • VERO's hosting and infrastructure providers, for encrypted/protected account, routing and service

    • operations.

  • Mobile network operators and recipient networks, when you place calls or send SMS/MMS messages.
  • External apps selected by you, such as WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or Telegram, when you choose to

    • open that service.

  • Professional advisers, authorities or courts where required by law or reasonably necessary to

    • protect rights, users and service security.

  • A successor organization in a genuine merger, acquisition or transfer, subject to applicable law

    • and continued protection of personal data.

We require service providers acting for VERO to handle data only for the contracted service and with appropriate safeguards.

RETENTION

VERO aims to retain information only for as long as needed for the stated purpose

  • Local contacts, call logs and SMS/MMS remain controlled by the device, Android databases and the

    • user. VERO's local preferences remain until deleted, cleared or uninstalled.

  • Call-reason delivery is temporary and retained only as long as necessary to route the reason to the

    • recipient.

  • Active account identifiers, verified-number records, device records, FCM routing and entitlement

    • history remain while the account is active and as needed to provide or restore service.

  • Revoked device tokens and sessions are disabled promptly and deleted or irreversibly anonymized

    • according to the production retention schedule.

  • Minimal security and anti-abuse events are retained for 180 days, or longer only when required for an active security investigation or by law.
  • Support requests are retained for up to 12 months after resolution unless longer

    • retention is needed for a dispute or legal obligation.

  • After an approved account-deletion request, VERO will remove or irreversibly anonymize associated

    • live account data within 30 days, except information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, payment records or legal compliance. Protected backups are overwritten within 90 days.

Google independently applies its retention periods to Firebase data. Google states that Firebase Authentication keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks and removes deleted authentication data from live and backup systems within up to 180 days after the Firebase customer initiates deletion.

Retention schedules are reviewed periodically and may be extended only where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal compliance.

SECURITY

VERO uses measures intended to protect personal data, including HTTPS in transit, protected lookup identifiers, encryption for sensitive server fields where implemented, hashed credentials, access controls, separated secrets, limited logging, backups and session/device revocation.

No system is completely secure. Users should protect their device, SIM, screen lock and Google account and report suspected unauthorized use promptly.

YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Decline optional permissions or disable optional features.
  • Change VERO's default Phone or SMS role through Android settings.
  • Disable missed-call SMS fallback, voice commands, notifications or other available settings.
  • Access and correct account information presented in VERO.
  • Request information about processing, correction, erasure or account deletion.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting earlier lawful processing.
  • Submit a grievance or complaint to the contact below and, where applicable, a competent authority.

Some requests require fresh OTP or equivalent verification to protect the account. We will not expose another person's account or data merely because a requester knows the phone number.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Users may request deletion from VERO Settings and through the public account-deletion page

https://vero-dialer.github.io/vero/delete-account.html

Requests may be initiated through the account-deletion page, the in-app verified deletion flow, or the VERO support form: https://forms.gle/sAJ4GXPtWhqAkwHa9

Deletion will revoke active sessions and online routing and remove associated server data according to the retention section. Uninstalling VERO does not by itself delete the Firebase/VERO account. Deleting the VERO account does not automatically erase call logs, SMS/MMS or contacts held by Android or another app.

CHILDREN

If you believe a child has provided personal data without required authorization, contact us so the matter can be reviewed and addressed.
